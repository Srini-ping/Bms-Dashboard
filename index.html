BMS Dashboard Overview

A Building Management System (BMS) is a centralized platform used to monitor, control, and manage the electrical and mechanical systems of a building or data center. It integrates various systems such as UPS, HVAC, power distribution, lighting, fire alarm, DG sets, and environmental monitoring into a single interface.

The primary purpose of a BMS is to improve operational efficiency, reduce energy consumption, enhance equipment reliability, and provide real-time monitoring with alarms and reports. Operators can quickly identify faults, monitor system performance, and perform preventive maintenance to ensure uninterrupted operation.

    UPS System Monitoring

    A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) is a critical power protection system that provides instant backup power during electrical failures or voltage disturbances. It ensures continuous operation of essential equipment such as servers, network devices, data centers, medical equipment, and industrial control systems.

    Power Distribution System

    The Power Distribution System is responsible for delivering electrical power safely and efficiently from the main power source to all equipment and facilities within a building. It includes transformers, switchgear, panels, circuit breakers, bus ducts, and distribution boards.

    HVAC System Monitoring

    The Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) system is a critical part of any commercial building and data center. Its primary function is to maintain a comfortable and controlled indoor environment by regulating temperature, humidity, airflow, and air quality.

    DG Set (Diesel Generator)

    A Diesel Generator (DG Set) provides reliable backup power during utility power failures, ensuring uninterrupted operation of critical equipment and facilities. Regular maintenance includes checking fuel levels, engine oil, coolant, and overall generator performance.
